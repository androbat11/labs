## Algorithmic Thinking Framework


* Understand & Constrain: Clarify input/output, sclae of $N$, negative numbers, duplicates, and constraints that hint at target complexity.
* State Invariants & Representation: Determine what data structure anturally models the problem state (Hash map for O(1) lookups, Heap for streaming min/max, Monotonic stack for next-greater, Graph for relatuionship)
* Strategy & Pattern Matching: Identify the algorithmic paradigm (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Fast & Slow pointers, Topological Sort, Dynamic Programming)
* Dry Run & Invarient Tracing: Walk through normal and corner cases (empty input, single element, all duplicates, extrema) before writing code.
* Implement, Verification & Complexity Audit: Write clean, modular code.
